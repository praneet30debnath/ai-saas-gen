# Flowlab — healthcare SaaS Business Plan

## Executive Summary

**Flowlab is the modern, AI-powered healthcare management platform that eliminates patient scheduling and helps businesses save 10+ hours per week.**

- **Market Size:** $28.5B (CAGR: 13.6%)
- **Target Audience:** Small to medium businesses in healthcare
- **Region:** global

## Competitive Moat

- Vertical-specific AI trained on healthcare data patterns

- Mobile-first design for on-the-go healthcare professionals
- Privacy-first: data never sold, GDPR/CCPA compliant
